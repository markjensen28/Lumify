## Adopted design direction

- Use high-detail modern fantasy pixel art with painterly shading, readable silhouettes, and a warm sense of discovery. Keep the existing brown and cream UI identity, cyan accents, pixel-style headings, and large clear controls (user brief, 2026-10-10).
- Place the [[docs/lore/LUMIFY_LORE_V2|Grand Library]] at the center of one compact connected world map, with all eight realm landmasses visible from the first view. Thin magical knowledge threads show their relationship to the Library (user brief, 2026-10-10).
- Show [[docs/lore/LUMIFY_LORE_V2|Vulcan Caverns]] as the initial available destination, with a visible active thread. Its first-route status is a story and presentation choice; it does not mean completing Science automatically unlocks another realm (user brief, 2026-10-10; [[docs/art-direction/LUMIFY_VISUAL_DIRECTION]]).
- Show [[docs/lore/LUMIFY_LORE_V2|Numeric Ruins]] and [[docs/lore/LUMIFY_LORE_V2|Reading Island]] as closer routes in light mist: recognizable silhouettes but unavailable and unnamed in the current player map. They belong to the capstone's three-subject scope, although only Science is playable in the audited prototype (user brief, 2026-10-10; [[docs/art-direction/LUMIFY_VISUAL_DIRECTION]]).
- Show the five future realms as distinct partial silhouettes under darker magical clouds. They are intentionally sealed worldbuilding regions, not planned playable content for this capstone (user brief, 2026-10-10).
- Show nameplates by default only for the Grand Library and unlocked realms. Hover, focus, click, or an accessibility list should communicate availability without revealing an unavailable realm's name in the present chapter (user brief, 2026-10-10; [[docs/art-direction/LUMIFY_VISUAL_DIRECTION]]).
- Geography clarification from the later user message: map realms are **ocean-grounded coasts and sea-level isles**, closely grouped around the Library, without floating rock platforms. The supplied floating-island art prompt is revised in [[docs/art-direction/LUMIFY_ASSET_AND_MUSIC_PROMPTS]] (user message, 2026-10-10).

## Production questions still open

- Final map arrangement after a silhouette and narrow-screen readability study.
- Final accessible narrow-screen presentation of the Library and eight realm groups, including Hibla's multiple islets.
- Whether a future realm name resolves at route opening or in a separate story beat. Current recommendation: reveal it with a playable route.

These are design approvals for later production, not blockers for preserving the direction in the documentation (as of 2026-10-10; [[docs/art-direction/LUMIFY_VISUAL_DIRECTION]]).
